An agent harness is the software layer that makes a language model a multi-step agent: it
assembles the model's input, executes its actions, and decides when to stop.
Prior surveys sample well-known systems, attach checkable evidence to design claims for at most
thirteen systems, report no coding reliability, and cannot tell an undocumented feature from an absent one, so they cannot relate design
to outcome. We report a pre-registered (\texttt{osf.io/ab2wn}) PRISMA 2020 review built on three
frameworks. \emph{Two-Tier Escalation Screening} yields a frame of 6,504 systems;
\emph{Evidence-Anchored Coding} codes 1,256 systems on 38 dimensions with a verbatim quote behind
every value; a \emph{Three-State Cell Contract} separates values, documented silence, and unsettled
cells. Weighting reverses the modal release artifact: repository-primary falls from 69.2\% of
1,252 coded systems to 33.3\%. Of 47,728 cells, 48.9\% record silence, from
24.9\% $\pm$ 1.2 (control loop) to 81.6\% $\pm$ 1.8 (sandbox), rates that are upper bounds, and 265 of 666 dimension pairs pass a
Benjamini--Hochberg-adjusted permutation test only when silence counts as a category. Only 53 of 1,256 systems (4.2\%) share a
benchmark, split, and base model with a peer, so the registered regression was not fitted;
multi-agent topology reaches $+0.88$ within-key standard deviations [$+0.38$, $+1.31$], attenuated by
drift but carried by keys that compare against recurring baselines (\rqXPOtherEffect{} \rqXPOtherCI{} on the
other \rqXPOtherKeys{}). A completed harvest of authors' ablations barely separates components: \rqDiscountSurviveZ{} of
\rqPoolableDims{} poolable dimensions pass a bias discount, with pooled gains of \rqEffectRangeSurviving{}
before a median discount of \rqMedianDiscountPct\%, all upper bounds; \rqZeroDimCount{} dimensions carry none.
Language-model instances screened and coded under a fixed protocol. Two model readings agree at mean
per-dimension $\kappa$ 0.784, but a human reading of 350 cells matches the model on 57.7\% [48.6, 66.9]:
on the audited silence dimensions the human confirms 41--63\% of the model's silent cells, and 117 of
the 148 disagreements lie in files the model's capped evidence bundle never contained.
