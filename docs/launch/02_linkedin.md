I have spent the past months reading how LLM agent harnesses are built: the software between a model and its task that assembles context, runs tools, and decides when to stop.

Today I am releasing HARNESS-DB: 1,256 harnesses from 2022 to 2026, coded on 38 design dimensions, with a verbatim quote and a re-openable locator behind every coded value. It comes from a pre-registered PRISMA 2020 review (osf.io/ab2wn) with a 6,504-system sampling frame.

What surprised me most is how much is not written down. 48.9% of the 47,728 cells are documented silence: the sources were read and do not say. Weighted to the field, the sandbox layer is 81.6% silent; the control loop is 24.9%.

I also checked the coding against a human reading. On 350 cells the two agreed 57.7% of the time, but 117 of the 148 disagreements were in files the coder was never sent. Where it had the evidence it was right on 202 of 233. So the silence rates are upper bounds, and I say so in the paper.

If you maintain a harness, your 38 cells are one search away, and a wrong-cell report with a quote is the most useful thing you can send me.
